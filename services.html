Services

One team of capabilities. A scope built for the task.

Vertex Solutions brings together commercial, technology and operational support. The exact deliverables are agreed for each engagement.

Market expansion and commercial growth

  • Startup expansion: support for Indian companies developing business in the United States.
  • Business development: introductions and coordination around relevant commercial relationships.
  • Sales development: outreach and customer acquisition activity based on an agreed target profile.
  • Fundraising support: preparation and coordination assistance; funding decisions remain with investors.

Vendor and manufacturing operations

  • Import-export coordination: supplier-side and vendor-side communication around trade workflows.
  • Manufacturing support: coordination for production-related requirements and operating needs.
  • Factory setup: support with planning and coordination for a manufacturing unit; statutory approvals remain with the relevant authorities and licensed advisers.
  • Manufacturer acquisition: commercial outreach to help manufacturing businesses identify potential customers.

Marketing, data and technology

  • Search visibility: SEO, answer engine optimisation and generative engine optimisation.
  • Marketing: digital campaigns and offline marketing activity.
  • AI integration: connecting AI tools to suitable business processes.
  • Agentic workflows: designing task-based systems for customer acquisition and other agreed processes.
  • Research-specific model training: support for narrowly defined research and training requirements.
  • Data brokerage: data sourcing and brokerage, subject to applicable permissions and legal requirements.
  • Recruitment: hiring support for agreed roles and teams.
  • Brand launches: support for planning and go-to-market activity.

How an engagement is scoped

Start with the objective, timeline, available resources and decision-maker. Vertex Solutions can then confirm whether the requirement fits its service scope and outline the proposed work. No service should be treated as a promise of funding, sales, approvals or a specific market outcome.
