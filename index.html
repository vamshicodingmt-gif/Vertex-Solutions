06 · Before we talk

A clear brief makes the first call useful.

You do not need a long deck. A few clear facts are enough to start.

State one clear goal. Name the product or service. Tell us the market you want to reach.

Say what your team has tried so far. Point to the main block. Add key dates.

Share a budget range, if known. Name the person who makes the call. Share links or files that explain the work.

Tell us what a good result looks like. A clear brief helps us set a fair scope.

  • One clear goal
  • Product or service details
  • Target market or region
  • Current progress and main block
  • Key dates and budget range
  • Decision-maker and useful links

08 · FAQ

Common questions.

What does Vertex Solutions do?

We support companies with market expansion, commercial development, vendor coordination, technology implementation, recruitment and manufacturing-related work.

Can you help an Indian startup expand to the US?

Yes. Support can include business introductions, outreach, sales activity and coordination around an expansion plan. The scope depends on the company's stage and requirements.

Do you implement AI in business workflows?

Services include integrating AI into existing processes and designing agentic workflows for customer acquisition and other agreed business tasks.

Do you work with manufacturers?

Yes. Work may include supplier and vendor coordination, manufacturing setup support and customer acquisition activity.

Do you publish client testimonials or certifications?

This website does not publish unverified testimonials or accreditation claims. Any project references or credentials should be confirmed directly before relying on them.
